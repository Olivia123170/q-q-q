# Thoughts and Feedback on the Thesis

> **Version:** v1.0.0 · **Released:** 2026-10-21
> English translation of the Chinese original《论文思考与意见反馈》.

The thesis has a clear theoretical framework, coherent logic, and detailed case analyses; it is well worth my study and reference. Nevertheless, from my personal perspective as a reader, I also noticed several problems.

1. The introduction lacks an account of the deeper motivation behind the choice of research material — for instance, the social significance of attending to this topic. Why are children positioned as witnesses of domestic violence? Beyond the perspective of translation, could reflections on social ethics also be incorporated into the selection of this topic, so that the research motivation becomes fuller and more profound?

2. In the theoretical foundation section, the content of 2.3 is too brief. It seems that the internal connection between the principles of informative-text translation and compensation strategies is analysed far too little, giving the impression that it is passed over in a single stroke.

3. The case analyses at each level are relatively few — especially in the discourse section, which contains only two examples and therefore feels somewhat thin. Some case analyses are rather far-fetched, and in my personal view some of the translation solutions are merely translational adjustments made out of Chinese usage or target-language convention, and cannot be counted as the application of translation compensation strategies.

4. The concluding section is relatively short. It focuses mainly on the conclusions drawn from the translation practice and on suggestions for further research, but lacks a systematic summary of reflections on the translation and of the limitations of the study.

5. In the selection of references, most of the references are outdated and there are few foreign-language references; in addition, many of the academic journals cited are not core journals. Moreover, eight master's theses are cited, which is a rather high proportion; more doctoral dissertations or high-level journal articles should be cited.

6. There are some problems with language expression in the article; for example, expressions such as *"you ji yu ci"* (有基于此) are not quite standard.
